How to read the badges

REAL VOTE
Decides the actual thing: passes a bill, confirms a nominee, overrides a veto.
PROCEDURAL
About how or whether to proceed. Can still be decisive: Senate cloture on a bill is often the real test.
AMENDMENT
A vote on changing the bill, not passing it.
ON THE FLOOR
What they're debating or have taken up. No vote yet.
COMING UP
Scheduled votes, cloture filings, when they'll meet next.
ROUTINE
Prayer, pledge, journal, quorum calls, adjournment.

House: a typical bill's path

1. The rule (H.Res.)
First the "previous question" vote, then adopting the rule. Both set debate terms and are party-line. Neither is the bill.
2. Debate + amendments
Amendment votes change the text.
3. Motion to recommit
The minority's last shot. Almost always fails.
4. Passage
The real vote. "Suspension" bills skip 1–3 and need two-thirds.

Senate: a typical bill's path

1. Cloture on motion to proceed
Needs 60 just to start debating.
2. Motion to proceed
Formally starts debate.
3. Amendments, waivers, tabling
Motions to waive budget rules need 60. A "yes" on tabling kills the item.
4. Cloture on the bill
The decisive 60-vote test.
5. Passage
The real vote (simple majority).
Nominees
Cloture (simple majority), then the confirmation vote.
